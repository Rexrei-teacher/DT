Popsicle Stick Truss Bridge

Work in a team. Design, test and build a strong bridge. Use as few sticks as you can.

Truss types you can use: Warren, Pratt, Howe, Camelback, or similar.

  • 5 kgMinimum weight your bridge must hold
  • 24–26 cmLength of the bridge
  • 0.5 kgMaximum weight of the bridge

Every class: write a diary entry

No diary entry means the class is not finished. Write these things:

  • Date and class number
  • What we did
  • How we did it
  • Where we got stuck
  • What was difficult for us
  • Plan for the next class
